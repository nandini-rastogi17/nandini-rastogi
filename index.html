Frequently Asked Questions

What age groups and grades do you teach?

For Vedic Mathematics and Scratch coding, I teach students between the ages of 7 and 16 (Grades 3 to 10). For collegiate or undergraduate students, I conduct larger NEP-aligned mental mathematics and problem-solving workshops.

How does the free diagnostic demo session work?

The free 20-minute session is held online via Zoom or Google Meet. I interact 1-on-1 with the child, assess how they solve mental arithmetic, and explain one fun Vedic math trick so they experience the difference right away.

Can you work with EdTech companies on a retainer or contract basis?

Yes. I collaborate with EdTech firms and schools for curriculum design, live batch training (100+ learners), and academic operational oversight.

What are the class timings and platform requirements?

Classes are held virtually over Zoom or Google Meet with interactive whiteboard support. Schedules are customized to student time zones across India, the Middle East, and other global regions.
